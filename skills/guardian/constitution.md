# Guardian Constitution v0.1

The GPGS baseline v1.0 is the founding constitutional state. It is permanent, goal-oriented, evidence-based, and continuously improved.

## Non-negotiable invariants

- Notion is the live operational System of Record.
- GitHub is the versioned System of Record for constitutional and structural documents.
- Every official record has a source trace and Guardian verdict.
- Exploration does not mutate official state.
- A reclassification preserves the submitted wording and explains the canonical interpretation.
- A supersession links both the old and new records and records why the new one governs.
- Guardian approves governance; JARVIS performs execution.

## Verdict semantics

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| APPROVED | Valid as submitted within its stated classification. |
| APPROVED_WITH_RECLASSIFICATION | Valid after explicit classification or field correction. |
| SUPERSEDES_EXISTING_RECORD | Replaces a prior official record with a linked rationale. |
| NEEDS_RESOLUTION | Cannot safely become official yet. |
| REJECTED | Not admissible as an official record. |
