# GPGS Architecture

The five roles are independent authorities:

| Role | Authority | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Career Strategist | ADVISE | Propose strategic goals and moves. |
| Evidence Auditor | VERIFY | Test claims against evidence. |
| Career Maturity Assessor | ASSESS | Score capability, evidence, and market relevance. |
| Guardian | APPROVE | Govern classification, conflicts, and official admission. |
| JARVIS | EXECUTE | Execute approved changes and maintain operations. |

The route is selective rather than a fixed chain, but the approval invariant is universal.
